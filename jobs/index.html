How it works

A clear process, before you travel

STEP 01

Tell us about yourself

Share your job type, experience, nationality and contact details. Do not send passport numbers in the first message.

STEP 02

Confirm the real offer

We discuss the current vacancy, employer, salary, hours, accommodation, meals and legal work arrangements.

STEP 03

Review before travel

Only proceed after you have checked the written terms and independently understood the visa and employment process.

Candidates do not pay KYRGYZ WORK a recruitment or placement fee. Recruitment services are employer-funded.
