Sewing and garment jobs

Garment and textile roles may include industrial sewing-machine operation and production-line work.

AVAILABILITY CONFIRMED ON REQUEST

Role overview

Garment and textile roles may include industrial sewing-machine operation and production-line work. The exact duties, location, salary, shift pattern and contract duration depend on the hiring employer and must be confirmed in writing before acceptance.

Skills employers may ask for

Sewing-machine experience, garment production knowledge, quality checking and shift readiness.

Before accepting a job

  • Confirm the employer's legal name and work location.
  • Get salary, payment schedule, hours, overtime and deductions in writing.
  • Confirm whether accommodation and meals are provided and what they include.
  • Understand contract duration, probation and termination terms.
  • Confirm the lawful work and permit process before travelling or starting work.
