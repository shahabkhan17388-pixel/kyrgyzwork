KYRGYZ WORK is an independent recruitment and workforce support service based in Kyrgyzstan, connecting employers with workers from Pakistan and South Asia.

What we do

We help employers describe their workforce needs and help candidates understand job categories, application steps and the conditions they should verify before accepting work.

How we work

We aim for clear communication about job roles, salary, working hours, accommodation, meals, deductions, recruitment scope and timelines. Availability and legal permissions must be confirmed for each case.

Our fee approach

Workers do not pay KYRGYZ WORK a recruitment or placement fee. Recruitment services are employer-funded. Government charges, travel and third-party expenses are separate and should be explained before any commitment.

Our role and limits

We are not a government agency, immigration authority or employer of every candidate shown on this website. We do not guarantee jobs, visas, permits, salaries or start dates.
