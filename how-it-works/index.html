A CLEAR PROCESS

Know the steps before you commit.فیصلہ کرنے سے پہلے مراحل سمجھیں۔Понятные этапы до принятия решения.

KYRGYZ WORK helps organise recruitment conversations. A submitted enquiry is not a confirmed job, permit, visa or travel approval.

01

1. Share the requirement

Workers share their name, nationality, skills, experience and contact details. Employers share roles, headcount, location, pay and project conditions.

02

2. Review the details

We discuss whether a matching opportunity may exist, and clarify the employer, worksite, salary, hours, accommodation, meals and deductions.

03

3. Confirm terms in writing

Before proceeding, check the written employment terms, recruitment scope, fees, responsibilities and any document or permit arrangements.

04

4. Coordinate next steps

Where agreed, we coordinate interviews, candidate communication, document checklists and travel planning.

Important: Do not pay unofficial intermediaries or travel based only on a verbal promise. Confirm the employer and legal requirements independently. Workers do not pay KYRGYZ WORK a recruitment or placement fee; government, travel and third-party costs are separate.
