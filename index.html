Short, clear answers for workers and employers. Rules can change, so always confirm the final details with us or the authorities.

Kyrgyzstan work visa for Pakistanis

Pakistani workers need a job offer, an official invitation and the correct documents before travelling. We prepare the invitation letter and your document file, and guide you through each step.

  • Valid passport and photos
  • CV and skill details
  • Job offer from a Kyrgyz employer
  • Invitation letter and visa application

Kyrgyzstan exit visa

If a foreigner stays after the visa expires, an overstay fine may apply and an exit visa may be needed to leave. The cost depends on how long the overstay lasted. Use our free calculator for a quick estimate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you help Pakistanis get a Kyrgyzstan work visa?

Yes. We prepare the invitation letter, the document checklist and the application support. Final decisions are made by the Kyrgyz authorities.

What is a Kyrgyzstan exit visa?

It is the document and fee that foreigners may need to leave Kyrgyzstan after their visa has expired. The cost depends on how long the overstay lasted. Use our calculator for an estimate.

Can you supply Pakistani workers to Kyrgyz employers?

Yes. We source and screen candidates from Pakistan and four other countries, then support the employer through documents, visa and arrival.

What do candidates pay for?

A fee for the invitation letter and work visa process. Contact us for the exact amount.

Are accommodation and meals free?

In most jobs, yes. Your job offer confirms it.

Which countries do you accept candidates from?

Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and Nepal.

Do you guarantee a visa?

No one can. We prepare and submit documents correctly; the Kyrgyz authorities decide.

My visa expired. What should I do?

Use our free calculator to estimate your protocol fine, then message us on WhatsApp.

Is the calculator result official?

No. It is an estimate. The final amount is set by the competent authority.
